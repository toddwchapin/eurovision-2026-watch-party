Help

A live scoring app for Eurovision watch parties. Rate each act as it performs, share scores with the room in real time, and see who's loving (or hating) the show.

Host or join

Enter your name and choose a contest. Leave the invite code blank and choose Start Party to host. Enter a host’s code (or open their invite link) and choose Join Party to join an existing party. Invites expire after seven days.

Your browser signs in anonymously; no password is needed. Another device gets a different identity even if you use the same name. Copy the invite link with the party-code button. Open Settings with the gear button to change your name or contest.

Rate an act

Choose Start Rating. Select a score from 0 (Terrible) to 5 (Amazing) for all five categories: Vocal quality, Performance flair, Song quality, Originality and Stagecraft. Zero is a deliberate score, not a default. Save stays disabled until all five are selected. The total is calculated automatically.

Notes are optional, up to 200 characters. Selections and notes are saved as a local draft, isolated by party, browser identity, contest and act. Drafts survive refresh but are never shared or included in results.

  • Save records a complete rating locally and queues only that act for sharing.
  • Skip moves on without voting and preserves the draft. At the end, choose Rate skipped acts to return. The completion message counts only saved ratings.
  • Clear clears the current form and its draft without deleting a previously saved vote.

The voting table shows your saved ratings, five category scores and total, sorted by total. Click a row or focus it and press Enter to edit. Top Pick is your own highest total.

Sharing and connection status

Pending means the rating is saved locally, not yet acknowledged. Shared means the server accepted it. Offline keeps changes on this device and retries when connected. Not shared means a write or access failed; use Retry, or Party settings to host/join again. Do not clear browser storage while votes are pending.

Different acts never replace one another. If two tabs edit the same act, the last write accepted by the server wins (including an offline edit that reconnects later). Check your results before closing the tabs.

Party Votes and Superlatives

Party Votes lists acts rated by members, ordered by average. Click or press Enter on an act to see individual totals and comments. Party Winner shows tied highest averages. Funniest Comment and the Superlatives page use the built-in humor scorer on current saved notes; drafts and removed notes do not count.

Superlatives include Biggest Hater, Biggest Lover, Most Verbose Comment, Most Verbose Overall, Wittiest and Most Incomplete. Score-based categories require at least three complete ratings.

Reset, leave and privacy

Reset votes in Settings deletes your shared ratings, local drafts and pending changes for the selected contest in this party. Other contests and other guests are preserved. Reconnect first; a failed reset leaves your data intact. Old offline tabs cannot restore pre-reset votes.

Leave party removes your shared name and votes in every contest and revokes membership. Local results remain on this browser; pending changes and drafts are canceled so they cannot reappear if you rejoin. Download your results from Settings before leaving. Leaving as host does not delete other guests or their party.

Only signed-in party members can read shared names, scores and notes. Each member can write only their own records. Treat an invite link like a shared secret: anyone with a valid invite can join. Drafts stay on your device. Clearing browser sign-in/storage loses access to the old anonymous identity.

Older party codes without secure metadata cannot be claimed automatically. Ask the host to create a new party. Legacy local vote files are retained, never uploaded automatically; see the rollout documentation for deliberate export/import options.

Keyboard access

Tab to a score group, then use arrow keys to choose a score. Space selects, and Enter activates buttons and rating rows. Settings dialogs keep focus inside; Escape closes Settings when its Close button is available and restores focus to the previous control.
