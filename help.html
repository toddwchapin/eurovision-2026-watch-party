Help

A live scoring app for Eurovision watch parties. Rate each act as it performs, share scores with the room in real time, and see who's loving (or hating) the show.

Getting started

  1. Enter your name. A modal appears on first load. Maximum 40 characters. Click the name pill in the top-right of the voter page to change it later.
  2. Create or join a party. A party is a shared session — everyone in it sees each other's votes live.
    • Create new — generates a 6-character code (letters and digits, no I, O, 1, or L to avoid confusion).
    • Join — type in the code someone shared with you. Case doesn't matter.
    • Invite link — anyone opening a URL like …/?party=ABC123 joins automatically. Use the "Copy link" button in the header to grab the link.
  3. Hit Start Rating when the show begins.

The voting page

Rating an act

Each act has its own card showing flag, country, performer, and song. Rate it on five 0–5 scales:

  • Vocal quality — how strong and controlled the vocals are.
  • Performance flair — energy, charisma, stage presence.
  • Song quality — how good the song itself is.
  • Originality — how fresh or distinctive the entry feels.
  • Stagecraft — costumes, visuals, choreography, production.

0 means Terrible, 5 means Amazing. The total (0–25) is calculated automatically. There's also a Replay-worthy? Yes/No toggle and an optional Notes field (max 200 characters) — drop quick observations or a "reminds me of…" line.

Save, Skip, Clear

  • Save — records your scores and moves to the next act. Votes sync to the rest of your party immediately.
  • Skip — moves on without recording a vote. The act stays in the queue; you can come back.
  • Clear — wipes the form but stays on the current act.

Acts are presented in performance order. If the running order isn't out yet, they're alphabetical. Acts not in the final are skipped entirely.

Editing a vote

Click any row in the Results Table to reopen that act's form pre-filled with your saved scores. Click Save to commit changes.

Results Table

Shows every act at least one party member has rated. Columns:

  • # — running order slot ("—" if unknown).
  • Act — flag, country, performer, song, and any note you left.
  • One column per member — their total (0–25), "—" if unrated. Your own column is bold gold with a ★.
  • Avg — average across everyone who's rated the act.

Click a column header to sort by it. Click again to flip direction. Default sort is Avg descending — highest-scoring acts on top.

Stats bar

  • Progress — how many acts you've rated out of the total.
  • Your Average — your mean total across all your rated acts.
  • Top Pick — the act with the highest party-wide average.

Settings & reset

The footer has links to rename yourself and to "Reset all my votes" — that erases your votes everywhere (device + party), with a confirmation step. Other members' votes are untouched.

Party Votes page

An overview of the whole party's scores for the current show.

  • Party Winner stat — the act with the highest average across everyone. Ties show every winner.
  • Funniest Comment stat (when present) — surfaces the wittiest note left on any vote.
  • Table — one row per act anyone rated, sorted by average score descending. Click a row to expand it inline and see each member's total and any comment they left for that act.

Party Trivia page

Superlatives based on everyone's votes and comments. A member needs to have rated at least 3 acts before they show up in score-based categories.

  • Biggest Hater — lowest average score across their rated acts.
  • Biggest Lover — highest average score.
  • Most Verbose Comment — longest single note left on any vote.
  • Most Verbose Overall — most characters of comments combined.
  • Wittiest — funniest comment as judged by the built-in humor scorer.
  • Most Incomplete — has rated something but skipped the most acts.

Privacy & data

  • Your name, party code, and votes are stored locally on this device.
  • If you're in a party, your name and saved votes are also written to Firebase so other members see them in real time. Only saved votes sync — in-progress selections aren't shared.
  • Reset removes your votes from both this device and the party.

Known issues

On iOS Safari and some other mobile WebKit browsers, the 0–5 score buttons may not register taps reliably — votes can save as all zeros. Desktop Chrome, Firefox, and Edge are not affected. A fix is in progress.
